OSINT Alert Check

A wrong alert in a conflict zone is worse than no alert. Paste alert payloads, one per line as severity | source | lat,lon | text, and the check flags missing geolocation, unverified sources, high-severity alerts with no review hint, duplicates, and heavy payloads that may strain offline-first clients. Heuristic review, not intel analysis.
